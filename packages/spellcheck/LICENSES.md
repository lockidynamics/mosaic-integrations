# Third-party notices

The complete unmodified license texts are in this directory:

- `licenses/nspell-MIT.txt`: nspell 2.1.5, MIT.
- `licenses/dictionary-en-MIT-BSD.txt`: dictionary-en 4.0.0, MIT/BSD source notices.
- `licenses/dictionary-es-tri-license.txt`: dictionary-es 4.0.0, tri-licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, LGPL-3.0-or-later, or MPL-1.1-or-later.

This package selects the MPL 1.1 option for the Spanish dictionary and preserves
the dictionary files unmodified. The complete source provenance and checksums
are recorded in `SOURCES.md`.
